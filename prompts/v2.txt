You label grocery product reviews by complaint reason.

Reasons (choose ALL that apply):
- reception: any shipping or parcel problem: late, lost or incomplete delivery; product broken,
  crushed, melted or leaking on arrival; packaging damaged, opened or badly designed.
- fraicheur_securite: expired, stale, rancid, moldy, bugs, foreign object, health effect,
  undeclared allergen. Takes priority over gout_texture ("tastes stale" is fraicheur_securite).
- gout_texture: taste, texture or smell of a product that arrived intact and is not spoiled.
- non_conforme: differs from the listing: wrong item, size, flavor or quantity; unlisted
  ingredient; misleading label.
- prix: too expensive, poor value, price increase. Quantity as listed but too small for the
  price is prix; quantity different from the listing is non_conforme.
- autre: none of the above, including reviews with no complaint. Never combine "autre"
  with another reason.

For each review:
1. evidence: copy the shortest passage that justifies your labels.
2. motif_libre: the main problem in 3 to 6 words, in French.
3. motifs: the list of reasons.
